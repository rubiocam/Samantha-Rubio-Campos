About Me

My name is Samantha Rubio-Campos. I am currently a Junior studying Computer Science with an emphasis in game design.
As a Computer Science (Games) student at the University of Southern California, I combine my technical foundation in computer science with the creative freedom of game design. My multidisciplinary background also spans marketing, user research, and 2D art, allowing me to approach problems from both analytical and creative perspectives.
Outside of academics, I’m passionate about music, dance, and cinematography, which are the creative outlets that continually inspire my approach to storytelling in games. I aim to craft interactive experiences that are not only emotionally resonant but also expand the boundaries of how stories can be told through game play.
